# Problem record

Use only relevant fields. Keep the learner's first wording before assistant suggestions.

- Decision ID / date / owner / mode:
- Current question and information cutoff:
- Learner's original problem statement:
- Actor, triggering situation, and job to complete:
- Current workflow or alternative, including doing nothing:
- Observed consequence; frequency/severity if actually known:
- Evidence IDs; distinguish observation, report, inference, and unknown:
- Suspected causes and competing explanations:
- Desired outcome and how it would be observed:
- Constraints and non-goals:
- Most decision-critical unknown; why it matters:
- Next evidence needed; who can supply it:
- G1: pending / confirmed / revise; owner, date, scope; SIMULATED if replay:

Coach question: “如果不能使用你提出的方案，你会怎样描述这个问题？” Wait for the learner; do not fill their answer.
