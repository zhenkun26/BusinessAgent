## Reading the assistance labels

- **Independently proposed reasoning:** the learner supplies a specific idea that the coach has not already supplied. A topic may still have been prompted, and prior exposure remains relevant.
- **Prompted extension:** the coach names a gap or evaluation dimension, then the learner supplies the details. Credit the proposed details without crediting independent discovery of the gap.
- **After historical reveal:** the learner revises a belief after receiving the case outcome. This is revision using evidence, not original root-cause discovery.
- **Synthetic transfer:** the learner responds to explicitly invented changed constraints. This demonstrates reasoning in that exercise, not success in a live project.
- **Not yet demonstrated:** a point appears only in coach feedback, or the necessary behavior/evidence was not observed. An unanswered exercise is not failure.

These are interpretation labels applied to specific answers, not fixed traits of the learner. The session maps also use the capability levels in the coaching contract.
