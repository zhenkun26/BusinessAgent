# Design

Use third_party/licenses/ as a retained source-notice location. Bind every scoped file to its SHA256, official source URL and applicable evidence kind. Preserve official license text bytes. Keep original embedded copyright statements separately from current upstream header variants.

For generated tooling, bind archive integrity and exact static official renderer/template matching; do not execute the downloaded package. For fonts, admit only the 89 exact official byte matches to the notice-added evidence group. Record the other 128 embedded OFL declarations and version/table comparison limits without claiming exact binary origin or completed clearance. Different bytes are not an infringement determination.

All records remain evidence, not a new acceptance status. The controlled inventory/checker remains untouched. A future distribution must copy the notices with the actual distributed assets, and any future evidence-status schema requires separate fail-closed design and tests.

## Refined license evidence interpretation

The initial 89 exact-byte-match group remains distinct. For the other 128 files, OFL
allows modification, subsetting and format conversion subject to its conditions; a
different hash is not a redistribution prohibition. The retained unresolved-source-map
filename now refers to incompletely reconstructed exact distribution lineage, not an
unsupported blanket lack of license. Record conditional applicability and obligations,
not absolute rights clearance. Keep original and retrieved upstream copyright notices.
