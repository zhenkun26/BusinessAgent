# Open-source readiness

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reproducible isolated starter
A new contributor SHALL be given a verified command and prerequisites for an isolated starter using synthetic data. Instructions SHALL identify actual services, network exposure, persistent outputs and a non-destructive stop procedure.

#### Scenario: Follow the quickstart
- **WHEN** a new contributor follows the documented command on a supported environment
- **THEN** observed startup and a synthetic smoke check match the recorded candidate evidence without production credentials or external business writes

### Requirement: Accurate capability and rights disclosure
Documentation SHALL distinguish implemented code, executed checks, substitute providers and unaccepted real integration. Unknown provenance SHALL NOT be represented as cleared rights.

#### Scenario: Demo-only provider
- **WHEN** an endpoint or answer uses a synthetic substitute
- **THEN** the guide labels it and does not claim real API/model/RAG or enterprise UAT acceptance

### Requirement: Conditional stage-one merge
Merge SHALL require the agreed stage-one evidence, resolved material distribution blockers and current candidate checks. Merge SHALL NOT imply deployment or later-stage completion.

#### Scenario: Missing rights or unverified startup
- **WHEN** a material source permission or documented startup result remains unverified
- **THEN** the missing evidence is reported and conditional merge is withheld
