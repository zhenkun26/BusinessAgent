# Prepare open-source stage one

## Why
The owner clarified that this is a personal open-source project aiming at enterprise use. The immediate milestone is open-source usability, followed by enterprise pilot evaluation, a real-environment pilot, and production use. Formal enterprise UAT is not silently required for this first milestone and is not claimed by synthetic checks.

## Scope
Provide a verified, isolated starter using synthetic data; disclose implemented capabilities, demo substitutes and unaccepted areas. Review source and license evidence without treating unknown rights as cleared. Bind verification to the merge candidate. Preserve the approval/requester-confirmation and human-hold rules.

## Merge boundary
Conditional merge requires verified stage-one usability, acceptable source/redistribution evidence for what is delivered, accurate limitations, independent review and green checks for the exact candidate. Unknown source rights remain a blocker requiring resolution rather than a fabricated permission. No deployment or real-enterprise acceptance is authorized by merge.
